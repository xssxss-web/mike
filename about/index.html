WHO WE ARE

A Local Sourcing Team Based in Yiwu, China

MikeOK is operated by Yiwu Gongbei Trading Firm, a sourcing company based in Yiwu International Trade City, the world's largest wholesale marketplace.

For more than 10 years, we have helped international buyers source products, connect with reliable suppliers, verify factories, inspect quality and coordinate exports across China.

Our team works directly with factories, market suppliers and logistics partners every day, allowing us to provide practical sourcing solutions and fast local support for businesses worldwide.

Whether you are searching for new products, comparing suppliers or managing production and shipments, we act as your trusted sourcing partner on the ground in China.
